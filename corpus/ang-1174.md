**ਪਰਪੰਚ ਵੇਖਿ ਰਹਿਆ ਵਿਸਮਾਦੁ ॥**

*parapa(n)ch vekh rahiaa visamaad ||*

Gazing upon the wonder of God's Creation, I am wonder-struck and amazed.
