<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਆਦੇਸੁ ਤਿਸੈ ਆਦੇਸੁ ॥**

*aades tisai aades ||*

I bow to Him, I humbly bow.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਆਦਿ ਅਨੀਲੁ ਅਨਾਦਿ ਅਨਾਹਤਿ ਜੁਗੁ ਜੁਗੁ ਏਕੋ ਵੇਸੁ ॥੨੯॥**

*aad aneel anaad anaahat jug jug eko ves ||29||*

The Primal One, the Pure Light, without beginning, without end. Throughout all the ages, He is One and the Same. ||29||

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਏਕਾ ਮਾਈ ਜੁਗਤਿ ਵਿਆਈ ਤਿਨਿ ਚੇਲੇ ਪਰਵਾਣੁ ॥**

*ekaa maaee jugat viaaee tin chele paravaan ||*

The One Divine Mother conceived and gave birth to the three deities.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਇਕੁ ਸੰਸਾਰੀ ਇਕੁ ਭੰਡਾਰੀ ਇਕੁ ਲਾਏ ਦੀਬਾਣੁ ॥**

*ik sa(n)saaree ik bha(n)daaree ik laae deebaan ||*

One, the Creator of the World; One, the Sustainer; and One, the Destroyer.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਜਿਵ ਤਿਸੁ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਿਵੈ ਚਲਾਵੈ ਜਿਵ ਹੋਵੈ ਫੁਰਮਾਣੁ ॥**

*jiv tis bhaavai tivai chalaavai jiv hovai phuramaan ||*

He makes things happen according to the Pleasure of His Will. Such is His Celestial Order.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਓਹੁ ਵੇਖੈ ਓਨਾ ਨਦਰਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਬਹੁਤਾ ਏਹੁ ਵਿਡਾਣੁ ॥**

*ohu vekhai onaa nadar na aavai bahutaa ehu vidaan ||*

He watches over all, but none see Him. How wonderful this is!
