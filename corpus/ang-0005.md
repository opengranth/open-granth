<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਆਖਣਿ ਸਭੁ ਕੋ ਆਖੈ ਇਕ ਦੂ ਇਕੁ ਸਿਆਣਾ ॥**

*naanak aakhan sabh ko aakhai ik doo ik siaanaa ||*

O Nanak, everyone speaks of Him, each one wiser than the rest.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਵਡਾ ਸਾਹਿਬੁ ਵਡੀ ਨਾਈ ਕੀਤਾ ਜਾ ਕਾ ਹੋਵੈ ॥**

*vadaa saahib vadee naaee keetaa jaa kaa hovai ||*

Great is the Master, Great is His Name. Whatever happens is according to His Will.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਜੇ ਕੋ ਆਪੌ ਜਾਣੈ ਅਗੈ ਗਇਆ ਨ ਸੋਹੈ ॥੨੧॥**

*naanak je ko aapau jaanai agai gaiaa na sohai ||21||*

O Nanak, one who claims to know everything shall not be decorated in the world hereafter. ||21||

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਪਾਤਾਲਾ ਪਾਤਾਲ ਲਖ ਆਗਾਸਾ ਆਗਾਸ ॥**

*paataalaa paataal lakh aagaasaa aagaas ||*

There are nether worlds beneath nether worlds, and hundreds of thousands of heavenly worlds above.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਓੜਕ ਓੜਕ ਭਾਲਿ ਥਕੇ ਵੇਦ ਕਹਨਿ ਇਕ ਵਾਤ ॥**

*oRak oRak bhaal thake ved kahan ik vaat ||*

The Vedas say that you can search and search for them all, until you grow weary.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਸਹਸ ਅਠਾਰਹ ਕਹਨਿ ਕਤੇਬਾ ਅਸੁਲੂ ਇਕੁ ਧਾਤੁ ॥**

*sahas athaarah kahan katebaa asuloo ik dhaat ||*

The scriptures say that there are 18,000 worlds, but in reality, there is only One Universe.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਲੇਖਾ ਹੋਇ ਤ ਲਿਖੀਐ ਲੇਖੈ ਹੋਇ ਵਿਣਾਸੁ ॥**

*lekhaa hoi ta likheeai lekhai hoi vinaas ||*

If you try to write an account of this, you will surely finish yourself before you finish writing it.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਵਡਾ ਆਖੀਐ ਆਪੇ ਜਾਣੈ ਆਪੁ ॥੨੨॥**

*naanak vadaa aakheeai aape jaanai aap ||22||*

O Nanak, call Him Great! He Himself knows Himself. ||22||

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->
**ਸਾਲਾਹੀ ਸਾਲਾਹਿ ਏਤੀ ਸੁਰਤਿ ਨ ਪਾਈਆ ॥**

*saalaahee saalaahi etee surat na paaeeaa ||*

The praisers praise the Lord, but they do not obtain intuitive understanding

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->
**ਨਦੀਆ ਅਤੈ ਵਾਹ ਪਵਹਿ ਸਮੁੰਦਿ ਨ ਜਾਣੀਅਹਿ ॥**

*nadeeaa atai vaah pavahi samu(n)d na jaaneeahi ||*

the streams and rivers flowing into the ocean do not know its vastness.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਸਮੁੰਦ ਸਾਹ ਸੁਲਤਾਨ ਗਿਰਹਾ ਸੇਤੀ ਮਾਲੁ ਧਨੁ ॥**

*samu(n)d saah sulataan girahaa setee maal dhan ||*

Even kings and emperors, with mountains of property and oceans of wealth

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਕੀੜੀ ਤੁਲਿ ਨ ਹੋਵਨੀ ਜੇ ਤਿਸੁ ਮਨਹੁ ਨ ਵੀਸਰਹਿ ॥੨੩॥**

*keeRee tul na hovanee je tis manahu na veesarahi ||23||*

-these are not even equal to an ant, who does not forget God. ||23||

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->

*a(n)t na siphatee kahan na a(n)t ||*

Endless are His Praises, endless are those who speak them.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਕਰਣੈ ਦੇਣਿ ਨ ਅੰਤੁ ॥**

*a(n)t na karanai den na a(n)t ||*

Endless are His Actions, endless are His Gifts.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਵੇਖਣਿ ਸੁਣਣਿ ਨ ਅੰਤੁ ॥**

*a(n)t na vekhan sunan na a(n)t ||*

Endless is His Vision, endless is His Hearing.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਜਾਪੈ ਕਿਆ ਮਨਿ ਮੰਤੁ ॥**

*a(n)t na jaapai kiaa man ma(n)t ||*

His limits cannot be perceived. What is the Mystery of His Mind?

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->
**ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਜਾਪੈ ਕੀਤਾ ਆਕਾਰੁ ॥**

*a(n)t na jaapai keetaa aakaar ||*

The limits of the created universe cannot be perceived.

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->

*a(n)t na jaapai paaraavaar ||*

Its limits here and beyond cannot be perceived.

---

Many struggle to know His limits,

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਤਾ ਕੇ ਅੰਤ ਨ ਪਾਏ ਜਾਹਿ ॥**

*taa ke a(n)t na paae jaahi ||*

but His limits cannot be found.

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਏਹੁ ਅੰਤੁ ਨ ਜਾਣੈ ਕੋਇ ॥**

*ehu a(n)t na jaanai koi ||*

No one can know these limits.

The more you say about them, the more there still remains to be said.

---

Only one as Great and as High as God

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->
**ਤਿਸੁ ਊਚੇ ਕਉ ਜਾਣੈ ਸੋਇ ॥**

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->

Only He Himself is that Great. He Himself knows Himself.

---

His Blessings are so abundant that there can be no written account of them.

---

<!-- LINE:11 -->

---

There are so many great, heroic warriors begging at the Door of the Infinite Lord.

---

---

<!-- LINE:12 -->

So many take and take again, and then deny receiving.

---

So many foolish consumers keep on consuming.

---

<!-- LINE:13 -->
**ਕੇਤਿਆ ਦੂਖ ਭੂਖ ਸਦ ਮਾਰ ॥**

So many endure distress, deprivation and constant abuse.

---

Even these are Your Gifts, O Great Giver!

---

Liberation from bondage comes only by Your Will.

---

<!-- LINE:14 -->

O Nanak, is the king of kings. ||25||

Priceless are His Virtues, Priceless are His Dealings.

Priceless are His Dealers, Priceless are His Treasures.

Priceless are those who come to Him, Priceless are those who buy from Him.

Priceless is the Divine Law of Dharma, Priceless is the Divine Court of Justice.

Priceless are His Blessings, Priceless is His Banner and Insignia.

Priceless is His Mercy, Priceless is His Royal Command.
