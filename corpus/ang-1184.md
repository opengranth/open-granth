**ਸੇ ਧਨਵੰਤ ਜਿਨ ਹਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਰਾਸਿ ॥**

*se dhanava(n)t jin har prabh raas ||*

They alone are rich, who have the Wealth of the Lord God.

Through the Word of the Guru's Shabad, sexual desire and anger are eradicated.
