**ਜਿ ਕਰਾਵੈ ਸੋ ਕਰਣਾ ॥**

*j karaavai so karanaa ||*

Whatever You cause us to do, we do.

*aath pahar araadhio har har man chi(n)diaa phal paaiaa || rahaau ||*
