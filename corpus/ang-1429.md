**ਨਿਜ ਕਰਿ ਦੇਖਿਓ ਜਗਤੁ ਮੈ ਕੋ ਕਾਹੂ ਕੋ ਨਾਹਿ ॥**

*nij kar dekhio jagat mai ko kaahoo ko naahi ||*

I had looked upon the world as my own, but no one belongs to anyone else.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਥਿਰੁ ਹਰਿ ਭਗਤਿ ਹੈ ਤਿਹ ਰਾਖੋ ਮਨ ਮਾਹਿ ॥੪੮॥**

*naanak thir har bhagat hai tih raakho man maahi ||48||*

O Nanak, only devotional worship of the Lord is permanent; enshrine this in your mind. ||48||

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਜਗ ਰਚਨਾ ਸਭ ਝੂਠ ਹੈ ਜਾਨਿ ਲੇਹੁ ਰੇ ਮੀਤ ॥**

*jag rachanaa sabh jhooth hai jaan lehu re meet ||*

The world and its affairs are totally false; know this well, my friend.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਕਹਿ ਨਾਨਕ ਥਿਰੁ ਨਾ ਰਹੈ ਜਿਉ ਬਾਲੂ ਕੀ ਭੀਤਿ ॥੪੯॥**

*kahi naanak thir naa rahai jiu baaloo kee bheet ||49||*

Says Nanak, it is like a wall of sand; it shall not endure. ||49||

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਰਾਮੁ ਗਇਓ ਰਾਵਨੁ ਗਇਓ ਜਾ ਕਉ ਬਹੁ ਪਰਵਾਰੁ ॥**

*raam gaio raavan gaio jaa kau bahu paravaar ||*

Raam Chand passed away, as did Raawan, even though he had lots of relatives.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਥਿਰੁ ਕਛੁ ਨਹੀ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਜਿਉ ਸੰਸਾਰੁ ॥੫੦॥**

*kahu naanak thir kachh nahee supane jiu sa(n)saar ||50||*

Says Nanak, nothing lasts forever; the world is like a dream. ||50||

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਚਿੰਤਾ ਤਾ ਕੀ ਕੀਜੀਐ ਜੋ ਅਨਹੋਨੀ ਹੋਇ ॥**

*chi(n)taa taa kee keejeeai jo anahonee hoi ||*

People become anxious, when something unexpected happens.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਇਹੁ ਮਾਰਗੁ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਕੋ ਨਾਨਕ ਥਿਰੁ ਨਹੀ ਕੋਇ ॥੫੧॥**

*ihu maarag sa(n)saar ko naanak thir nahee koi ||51||*

This is the way of the world, O Nanak; nothing is stable or permanent. ||51||

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਜੋ ਉਪਜਿਓ ਸੋ ਬਿਨਸਿ ਹੈ ਪਰੋ ਆਜੁ ਕੈ ਕਾਲਿ ॥**

*jo upajio so binas hai paro aaj kai kaal ||*

Whatever has been created shall be destroyed; everyone shall perish, today or tomorrow.

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਹਰਿ ਗੁਨ ਗਾਇ ਲੇ ਛਾਡਿ ਸਗਲ ਜੰਜਾਲ ॥੫੨॥**

*naanak har gun gaai le chhaad sagal ja(n)jaal ||52||*

O Nanak, sing the Glorious Praises of the Lord, and give up all other entanglements. ||52||

*bal chhutakio ba(n)dhan pare kachhoo na hot upaai ||*

My strength is exhausted, and I am in bondage; I cannot do anything at all.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਅਬ ਓਟ ਹਰਿ ਗਜ ਜਿਉ ਹੋਹੁ ਸਹਾਇ ॥੫੩॥**

*kahu naanak ab ot har gaj jiu hohu sahaai ||53||*

Says Nanak, now, the Lord is my Support; He will help me, as He did the elephant. ||53||

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਬਲੁ ਹੋਆ ਬੰਧਨ ਛੁਟੇ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਹੋਤ ਉਪਾਇ ॥**

*bal hoaa ba(n)dhan chhute sabh kichh hot upaai ||*

My strength has been restored, and my bonds have been broken; now, I can do everything.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਤੁਮਰੈ ਹਾਥ ਮੈ ਤੁਮ ਹੀ ਹੋਤ ਸਹਾਇ ॥੫੪॥**

*naanak sabh kichh tumarai haath mai tum hee hot sahaai ||54||*

Nanak: everything is in Your hands, Lord; You are my Helper and Support. ||54||

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->
**ਸੰਗ ਸਖਾ ਸਭਿ ਤਜਿ ਗਏ ਕੋਊ ਨ ਨਿਬਹਿਓ ਸਾਥਿ ॥**

*sa(n)g sakhaa sabh taj gae kooo na nibahio saath ||*

My associates and companions have all deserted me; no one remains with me.

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->
**ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਇਹ ਬਿਪਤਿ ਮੈ ਟੇਕ ਏਕ ਰਘੁਨਾਥ ॥੫੫॥**

*kahu naanak ih bipat mai tek ek raghunaath ||55||*

Says Nanak, in this tragedy, the Lord alone is my Support. ||55||

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਨਾਮੁ ਰਹਿਓ ਸਾਧੂ ਰਹਿਓ ਰਹਿਓ ਗੁਰੁ ਗੋਬਿੰਦੁ ॥**

*naam rahio saadhoo rahio rahio gur gobi(n)d ||*

The Naam remains; the Holy Saints remain; the Guru, the Lord of the Universe, remains.

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਇਹ ਜਗਤ ਮੈ ਕਿਨ ਜਪਿਓ ਗੁਰ ਮੰਤੁ ॥੫੬॥**

*kahu naanak ih jagat mai kin japio gur ma(n)t ||56||*

Says Nanak, how rare are those who chant the Guru's Mantra in this world. ||56||

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->
**ਰਾਮ ਨਾਮੁ ਉਰ ਮੈ ਗਹਿਓ ਜਾ ਕੈ ਸਮ ਨਹੀ ਕੋਇ ॥**

*raam naam ur mai gahio jaa kai sam nahee koi ||*

I have enshrined the Lord's Name within my heart; there is nothing equal to it.

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->

*jih simarat sa(n)kat mitai daras tuhaaro hoi ||57||1||*

Upon this Plate, three things have been placed: Truth, Contentment and Contemplation.

One who eats it and enjoys it shall be saved.

This thing can never be forsaken; keep this always and forever in your mind.

---

*tam sa(n)saar charan lag tareeai sabh naanak braham pasaaro ||1||*

The dark world-ocean is crossed over, by grasping the Feet of the Lord; O Nanak, it is all the extension of God. ||1||

Salok, Fifth Mehl:

<!-- LINE:14 -->

*teraa keetaa jaato naahee maino jog keetoee ||*

I have not appreciated what You have done for me, Lord; only You can make me worthy.

---

<!-- LINE:15 -->

*mai niraguniaare ko gun naahee aape taras paioee ||*

I am unworthy - I have no worth or virtues at all. You have taken pity on me.

---

<!-- LINE:15 -->
**ਤਰਸੁ ਪਇਆ ਮਿਹਰਾਮਤਿ ਹੋਈ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਸਜਣੁ ਮਿਲਿਆ ॥**

*taras paiaa miharaamat hoee satigur sajan miliaa ||*

You took pity on me, and blessed me with Your Mercy, and I have met the True Guru, my Friend.

---

<!-- LINE:16 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਾਂ ਜੀਵਾਂ ਤਨੁ ਮਨੁ ਥੀਵੈ ਹਰਿਆ ॥੧॥**

*naanak naam milai taa(n) jeevaa(n) tan man theevai hariaa ||1||*

O Nanak, if I am blessed with the Naam, I live, and my body and mind blossom forth. ||1||
