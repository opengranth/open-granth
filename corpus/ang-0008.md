**ਸਰਮ ਖੰਡ ਕੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਰੂਪੁ ॥**

*saram kha(n)d kee baanee roop ||*

In the realm of humility, the Word is Beauty.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਤਿਥੈ ਘਾੜਤਿ ਘੜੀਐ ਬਹੁਤੁ ਅਨੂਪੁ ॥**

*tithai ghaaRat ghaReeai bahut anoop ||*

Forms of incomparable beauty are fashioned there.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->

*taa keeaa galaa katheeaa naa jaahi ||*

These things cannot be described.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->

*je ko kahai pichhai pachhutaai ||*

One who tries to speak of these shall regret the attempt.

---

*tithai ghaReeai surat mat man budh ||*

The intuitive consciousness, intellect and understanding of the mind are shaped there.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->

*tithai ghaReeai suraa sidhaa kee sudh ||36||*

The consciousness of the spiritual warriors and the Siddhas, the beings of spiritual perfection, are shaped there. ||36||

*karam kha(n)d kee baanee jor ||*

In the realm of karma, the Word is Power.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->

*tin mahi raam rahiaa bharapoor ||*

They are totally fulfilled, imbued with the Lord's Essence.

---

Myriads of Sitas are there, cool and calm in their majestic glory.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->

*taa ke roop na kathane jaahi ||*

Their beauty cannot be described.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->

*naa ohi marahi na thaage jaahi ||*

Neither death nor deception comes to those,

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->

within whose minds the Lord abides.

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->
**ਤਿਥੈ ਭਗਤ ਵਸਹਿ ਕੇ ਲੋਅ ॥**

*tithai bhagat vasahi ke lo ||*

The devotees of many worlds dwell there.

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->

*karahi ana(n)d sachaa man soi ||*

They celebrate; their minds are imbued with the True Lord.

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->

*sach kha(n)d vasai nira(n)kaar ||*

In the realm of Truth, the Formless Lord abides.

---

*kar kar vekhai nadar nihaal ||*

Having created the creation, He watches over it. By His Glance of Grace, He bestows happiness.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->

*tithai kha(n)d ma(n)dal varabha(n)d ||*

There are planets, solar systems and galaxies.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->

*je ko kathai ta a(n)t na a(n)t ||*

If one speaks of them, there is no limit, no end.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਤਿਥੈ ਲੋਅ ਲੋਅ ਆਕਾਰ ॥**

*tithai lo lo aakaar ||*

There are worlds upon worlds of His Creation.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->

*jiv jiv hukam tivai tiv kaar ||*

As He commands, so they exist.

---

*vekhai vigasai kar veechaar ||*

He watches over all, and contemplating the creation, He rejoices.

---

O Nanak, to describe this is as hard as steel! ||37||

---

In the crucible of love, melt the Nectar of the Name,

Day and night are the two nurses, in whose lap all the world is at play.

Good deeds and bad deeds-the record is read out in the Presence of the Lord of Dharma.

<!-- LINE:15 -->

*vaaje tere naad anek asa(n)khaa kete tere vaavanahaare ||*

The Sound-current of the Naad vibrates there for You, and countless musicians play all sorts of instruments there for You.

---

<!-- LINE:15 -->
**ਕੇਤੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਰਾਗ ਪਰੀ ਸਿਉ ਕਹੀਅਹਿ ਕੇਤੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਗਾਵਣਹਾਰੇ ॥**

*kete tere raag paree siu kaheeahi kete tere gaavanahaare ||*

There are so many Ragas and musical harmonies to You; so many minstrels sing hymns of You.

---

<!-- LINE:16 -->
**ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਪਵਣੁ ਪਾਣੀ ਬੈਸੰਤਰੁ ਗਾਵੈ ਰਾਜਾ ਧਰਮੁ ਦੁਆਰੇ ॥**

*gaavan tudhano pavan paanee baisa(n)tar gaavai raajaa dharam duaare ||*

Wind, water and fire sing of You. The Righteous Judge of Dharma sings at Your Door.

---

<!-- LINE:17 -->
**ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਚਿਤੁ ਗੁਪਤੁ ਲਿਖਿ ਜਾਣਨਿ ਲਿਖਿ ਲਿਖਿ ਧਰਮੁ ਬੀਚਾਰੇ ॥**

*gaavan tudhano chit gupat likh jaanan likh likh dharam beechaare ||*

Chitr and Gupt, the angels of the conscious and the subconscious who keep the record of actions, and the Righteous Judge of Dharma who reads this record, sing of You.

---

<!-- LINE:17 -->
**ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਈਸਰੁ ਬ੍ਰਹਮਾ ਦੇਵੀ ਸੋਹਨਿ ਤੇਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਸਵਾਰੇ ॥**

*gaavan tudhano eesar brahamaa devee sohan tere sadaa savaare ||*

Shiva, Brahma and the Goddess of Beauty, ever adorned by You, sing of You.

---

<!-- LINE:18 -->
**ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਇੰਦ੍ਰ ਇੰਦ੍ਰਾਸਣਿ ਬੈਠੇ ਦੇਵਤਿਆ ਦਰਿ ਨਾਲੇ ॥**

*gaavan tudhano i(n)dr i(n)draasan baithe devatiaa dar naale ||*

Indra, seated on His Throne, sings of You, with the deities at Your Door.

---

<!-- LINE:19 -->
**ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਸਿਧ ਸਮਾਧੀ ਅੰਦਰਿ ਗਾਵਨਿ ਤੁਧਨੋ ਸਾਧ ਬੀਚਾਰੇ ॥**

*gaavan tudhano sidh samaadhee a(n)dar gaavan tudhano saadh beechaare ||*

The Siddhas in Samaadhi sing of You; the Saadhus sing of You in contemplation.
