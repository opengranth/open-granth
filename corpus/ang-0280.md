**ਨਾਨਕ ਸੰਤ ਭਾਵੈ ਤਾ ਓਇ ਭੀ ਗਤਿ ਪਾਹਿ ॥੨॥**

The slanderer of the Saint contracts all diseases.

To slander a Saint is the worst sin of sins.

The slanderer of the Saint is nobody's friend.
