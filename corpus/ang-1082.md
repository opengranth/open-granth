<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਆਪੇ ਸੂਰਾ ਅਮਰੁ ਚਲਾਇਆ ॥**

*aape sooraa amar chalaaiaa ||*

You Yourself are the hero, exerting Your regal power.

---

<!-- LINE:1 -->
**ਆਪੇ ਸਿਵ ਵਰਤਾਈਅਨੁ ਅੰਤਰਿ ਆਪੇ ਸੀਤਲੁ ਠਾਰੁ ਗੜਾ ॥੧੩॥**

*aape siv varataaeean a(n)tar aape seetal thaar gaRaa ||13||*

You Yourself spread peace within; You are cool and icy calm. ||13||

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਜਿਸਹਿ ਨਿਵਾਜੇ ਗੁਰਮੁਖਿ ਸਾਜੇ ॥**

*jisahi nivaaje guramukh saaje ||*

One whom You bless and make Gurmukh

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਨਾਮੁ ਵਸੈ ਤਿਸੁ ਅਨਹਦ ਵਾਜੇ ॥**

*naam vasai tis anahad vaaje ||*

the Naam abides within him, and the unstruck sound current vibrates for him.

---

<!-- LINE:2 -->
**ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਸੁਖੁ ਤਿਸ ਹੀ ਠਕੁਰਾਈ ਤਿਸਹਿ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਜਮੁ ਨੇੜਾ ॥੧੪॥**

*tis hee sukh tis hee thakuraaee tisahi na aavai jam neRaa ||14||*

He is peaceful, and he is the master of all; the Messenger of Death does not even approach him. ||14||

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਕੀਮਤਿ ਕਾਗਦ ਕਹੀ ਨ ਜਾਈ ॥**

*keemat kaagad kahee na jaaee ||*

His value cannot be described on paper.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਕਹੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਬੇਅੰਤ ਗੁਸਾਈ ॥**

*kahu naanak bea(n)t gusaaee ||*

Says Nanak, the Lord of the world is infinite.

---

<!-- LINE:3 -->
**ਆਦਿ ਮਧਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਸੋਈ ਹਾਥਿ ਤਿਸੈ ਕੈ ਨੇਬੇੜਾ ॥੧੫॥**

*aad madh a(n)t prabh soee haath tisai kai nebeRaa ||15||*

In the beginning, in the middle and in the end, God exists. Judgement is in His Hands alone. ||15||

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਤਿਸਹਿ ਸਰੀਕੁ ਨਾਹੀ ਰੇ ਕੋਈ ॥**

*tisahi sareek naahee re koee ||*

No one is equal to Him.

---

<!-- LINE:4 -->
**ਕਿਸ ਹੀ ਬੁਤੈ ਜਬਾਬੁ ਨ ਹੋਈ ॥**

*kis hee butai jabaab na hoee ||*

No one can stand up against Him by any means.

---

<!-- LINE:5 -->
**ਨਾਨਕ ਕਾ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਆਪੇ ਆਪੇ ਕਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਵੇਖੈ ਚੋਜ ਖੜਾ ॥੧੬॥੧॥੧੦॥**

*naanak kaa prabh aape aape kar kar vekhai choj khaRaa ||16||1||10||*

Nanak's God is Himself all-in-all. He creates and stages and watches His wondrous plays. ||16||1||10||

---

**ਮਾਰੂ ਮਹਲਾ ੫ ॥**

*maaroo mahalaa 5 ||*

Maaroo, Fifth Mehl:

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਅਚੁਤ ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਪਰਮੇਸੁਰ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ ॥**

*achut paarabraham paramesur a(n)tarajaamee ||*

The Supreme Lord God is imperishable, the Transcendent Lord, the Inner-knower, the Searcher of hearts.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਮਧੁਸੂਦਨ ਦਾਮੋਦਰ ਸੁਆਮੀ ॥**

*madhusoodan daamodar suaamee ||*

He is the Slayer of demons, our Supreme Lord and Master.

---

<!-- LINE:6 -->
**ਰਿਖੀਕੇਸ ਗੋਵਰਧਨ ਧਾਰੀ ਮੁਰਲੀ ਮਨੋਹਰ ਹਰਿ ਰੰਗਾ ॥੧॥**

*rikheekes govaradhan dhaaree muralee manohar har ra(n)gaa ||1||*

The Supreme Rishi, the Master of the sensory organs, the uplifter of mountains, the joyful Lord playing His enticing flute. ||1||

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਮੋਹਨ ਮਾਧਵ ਕ੍ਰਿਸ੍ਨ ਮੁਰਾਰੇ ॥**

*mohan maadhav krisn muraare ||*

The Enticer of Hearts, the Lord of wealth, Krishna, the Enemy of ego.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਜਗਦੀਸੁਰ ਹਰਿ ਜੀਉ ਅਸੁਰ ਸੰਘਾਰੇ ॥**

*jagadeesur har jeeu asur sa(n)ghaare ||*

The Lord of the Universe, the Dear Lord, the Destroyer of demons.

---

<!-- LINE:7 -->
**ਜਗਜੀਵਨ ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਠਾਕੁਰ ਘਟ ਘਟ ਵਾਸੀ ਹੈ ਸੰਗਾ ॥੨॥**

*jagajeevan abinaasee thaakur ghat ghat vaasee hai sa(n)gaa ||2||*

The Life of the World, our eternal and ever-stable Lord and Master dwells within each and every heart, and is always with us. ||2||

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->
**ਧਰਣੀਧਰ ਈਸ ਨਰਸਿੰਘ ਨਾਰਾਇਣ ॥**

*dharaneedhar ees narasi(n)gh naaraain ||*

The Support of the Earth, the man-lion, the Supreme Lord God.

---

<!-- LINE:8 -->
**ਦਾੜਾ ਅਗ੍ਰੇ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮਿ ਧਰਾਇਣ ॥**

*daaRaa agre pritham dharaain ||*

The Protector who tears apart demons with His teeth, the Upholder of the earth.

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਬਾਵਨ ਰੂਪੁ ਕੀਆ ਤੁਧੁ ਕਰਤੇ ਸਭ ਹੀ ਸੇਤੀ ਹੈ ਚੰਗਾ ॥੩॥**

*baavan roop keeaa tudh karate sabh hee setee hai cha(n)gaa ||3||*

O Creator, You assumed the form of the pygmy to humble the demons; You are the Lord God of all. ||3||

---

<!-- LINE:9 -->
**ਸ੍ਰੀ ਰਾਮਚੰਦ ਜਿਸੁ ਰੂਪੁ ਨ ਰੇਖਿਆ ॥**

*sree raamacha(n)d jis roop na rekhiaa ||*

You are the Great Raam Chand, who has no form or feature.

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->
**ਬਨਵਾਲੀ ਚਕ੍ਰਪਾਣਿ ਦਰਸਿ ਅਨੂਪਿਆ ॥**

*banavaalee chakrapaan daras anoopiaa ||*

Adorned with flowers, holding the chakra in Your hand, Your form is incomparably beautiful.

---

<!-- LINE:10 -->
**ਸਹਸ ਨੇਤ੍ਰ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਹੈ ਸਹਸਾ ਇਕੁ ਦਾਤਾ ਸਭ ਹੈ ਮੰਗਾ ॥੪॥**

*sahas netr moorat hai sahasaa ik daataa sabh hai ma(n)gaa ||4||*

You have thousands of eyes, and thousands of forms. You alone are the Giver, and all are beggars of You. ||4||

---

<!-- LINE:11 -->
**ਭਗਤਿ ਵਛਲੁ ਅਨਾਥਹ ਨਾਥੇ ॥**

*bhagat vachhal anaathah naathe ||*

You are the Lover of Your devotees, the Master of the masterless.

---

<!-- LINE:11 -->
**ਗੋਪੀ ਨਾਥੁ ਸਗਲ ਹੈ ਸਾਥੇ ॥**

*gopee naath sagal hai saathe ||*

The Lord and Master of the milk-maids, You are the companion of all.

---

<!-- LINE:11 -->
**ਬਾਸੁਦੇਵ ਨਿਰੰਜਨ ਦਾਤੇ ਬਰਨਿ ਨ ਸਾਕਉ ਗੁਣ ਅੰਗਾ ॥੫॥**

*baasudev nira(n)jan daate baran na saakau gun a(n)gaa ||5||*

O Lord, Immacuate Great Giver, I cannot describe even an iota of Your Glorious Virtues. ||5||

---

<!-- LINE:12 -->
**ਮੁਕੰਦ ਮਨੋਹਰ ਲਖਮੀ ਨਾਰਾਇਣ ॥**

*muka(n)d manohar lakhamee naaraain ||*

Liberator, Enticing Lord, Lord of Lakshmi, Supreme Lord God.

---

<!-- LINE:12 -->
**ਦ੍ਰੋਪਤੀ ਲਜਾ ਨਿਵਾਰਿ ਉਧਾਰਣ ॥**

*dropatee lajaa nivaar udhaaran ||*

Savior of Dropadi's honor.

---

<!-- LINE:12 -->
**ਕਮਲਾਕੰਤ ਕਰਹਿ ਕੰਤੂਹਲ ਅਨਦ ਬਿਨੋਦੀ ਨਿਹਸੰਗਾ ॥੬॥**

*kamalaaka(n)t karahi ka(n)toohal anad binodee nihasa(n)gaa ||6||*

Lord of Maya, miracle-worker, absorbed in delightful play, unattached. ||6||

---

<!-- LINE:13 -->
**ਅਮੋਘ ਦਰਸਨ ਆਜੂਨੀ ਸੰਭਉ ॥**

*amogh darasan aajoonee sa(n)bhau ||*

The Blessed Vision of His Darshan is fruitful and rewarding; He is not born, He is self-existent.

---

<!-- LINE:13 -->
**ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਜਿਸੁ ਕਦੇ ਨਾਹੀ ਖਉ ॥**

*akaal moorat jis kade naahee khau ||*

His form is undying; it is never destroyed.

---

<!-- LINE:14 -->
**ਅਬਿਨਾਸੀ ਅਬਿਗਤ ਅਗੋਚਰ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਤੁਝ ਹੀ ਹੈ ਲਗਾ ॥੭॥**

*abinaasee abigat agochar sabh kichh tujh hee hai lagaa ||7||*

O imperishable, eternal, unfathomable Lord, everything is attached to You. ||7||

---

<!-- LINE:14 -->
**ਸ੍ਰੀਰੰਗ ਬੈਕੁੰਠ ਕੇ ਵਾਸੀ ॥**

*sreera(n)g baiku(n)th ke vaasee ||*

The Lover of greatness, who dwells in heaven.

---

<!-- LINE:15 -->
**ਮਛੁ ਕਛੁ ਕੂਰਮੁ ਆਗਿਆ ਅਉਤਰਾਸੀ ॥**

*machh kachh kooram aagiaa autaraasee ||*

By the Pleasure of His Will, He took incarnation as the great fish and the tortoise.

---

<!-- LINE:15 -->
**ਕੇਸਵ ਚਲਤ ਕਰਹਿ ਨਿਰਾਲੇ ਕੀਤਾ ਲੋੜਹਿ ਸੋ ਹੋਇਗਾ ॥੮॥**

*kesav chalat karahi niraale keetaa loRahi so hoigaa ||8||*

The Lord of beauteous hair, the Worker of miraculous deeds, whatever He wishes, comes to pass. ||8||

---

<!-- LINE:16 -->
**ਨਿਰਾਹਾਰੀ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਸਮਾਇਆ ॥**

*niraahaaree niravair samaaiaa ||*

He is beyond need of any sustenance, free of hate and all-pervading.

---

<!-- LINE:16 -->
**ਧਾਰਿ ਖੇਲੁ ਚਤੁਰਭੁਜੁ ਕਹਾਇਆ ॥**

*dhaar khel chaturabhuj kahaaiaa ||*

He has staged His play; He is called the four-armed Lord.

---

<!-- LINE:16 -->
**ਸਾਵਲ ਸੁੰਦਰ ਰੂਪ ਬਣਾਵਹਿ ਬੇਣੁ ਸੁਨਤ ਸਭ ਮੋਹੈਗਾ ॥੯॥**

*saaval su(n)dar roop banaavahi ben sunat sabh mohaigaa ||9||*

He assumed the beautiful form of the blue-skinned Krishna; hearing His flute, all are fascinated and enticed. ||9||

---

<!-- LINE:17 -->
**ਬਨਮਾਲਾ ਬਿਭੂਖਨ ਕਮਲ ਨੈਨ ॥**

*banamaalaa bibhookhan kamal nain ||*

He is adorned with garlands of flowers, with lotus eyes.

---

<!-- LINE:17 -->
**ਸੁੰਦਰ ਕੁੰਡਲ ਮੁਕਟ ਬੈਨ ॥**

*su(n)dar ku(n)dal mukat bain ||*

His ear-rings, crown and flute are so beautiful.

---

<!-- LINE:17 -->
**ਸੰਖ ਚਕ੍ਰ ਗਦਾ ਹੈ ਧਾਰੀ ਮਹਾ ਸਾਰਥੀ ਸਤਸੰਗਾ ॥੧੦॥**

*sa(n)kh chakr gadaa hai dhaaree mahaa saarathee satasa(n)gaa ||10||*

He carries the conch, the chakra and the war club; He is the Great Charioteer, who stays with His Saints. ||10||

---

**ਪੀਤ ਪੀਤੰਬਰ ਤ੍ਰਿਭਵਣ ਧਣੀ ॥**

*peet peeta(n)bar tribhavan dhanee ||*

The Lord of yellow robes, the Master of the three worlds.

---

<!-- LINE:18 -->
**ਜਗੰਨਾਥੁ ਗੋਪਾਲੁ ਮੁਖਿ ਭਣੀ ॥**

*jaga(n)naath gopaal mukh bhanee ||*

The Lord of the Universe, the Lord of the world; with my mouth, I chant His Name.

---

<!-- LINE:18 -->
**ਸਾਰਿੰਗਧਰ ਭਗਵਾਨ ਬੀਠੁਲਾ ਮੈ ਗਣਤ ਨ ਆਵੈ ਸਰਬੰਗਾ ॥੧੧॥**

*saari(n)gadhar bhagavaan beethulaa mai ganat na aavai saraba(n)gaa ||11||*

The Archer who draws the bow, the Beloved Lord God; I cannot count all His limbs. ||11||

---

*nihaka(n)tak nihakeval kaheeai ||*

He is said to be free of anguish, and absolutely immaculate.

---

<!-- LINE:19 -->
**ਧਨੰਜੈ ਜਲਿ ਥਲਿ ਹੈ ਮਹੀਐ ॥**

*dhana(n)jai jal thal hai maheeai ||*

The Lord of prosperity, pervading the water, the land and the sky.
