**ਸੰਤ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ਪਰਮ ਪਦੁ ਪਾਇਆ ॥੨॥**

*sa(n)t prasaad param pad paaiaa ||2||*

By the Grace of the Saints, I have obtained the supreme status. ||2||

I have found peace, falling at the feet of His slaves.
