# Takedown

Open Granth is an independent, non-commercial seva project. This document describes how takedown requests are handled.

## How to request a takedown

Send requests to **contact@opengranth.org** with the subject line `[Open Granth Takedown]`.

Please include:

1. Your identity and authority to act for the rights holder.
2. The specific translation, passage, or work at issue (title, translator, edition, year).
3. The specific file paths or URLs where you believe the infringing material appears.
4. A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized.
5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the request is accurate.

## Commitments

1. Open Granth acknowledges any good-faith request promptly.
2. If the request is facially valid, the identified material is disabled promptly while the claim is evaluated. An internal timestamped copy of the removed material is preserved for record-keeping only.
3. Open Granth intends to publish a redacted ledger of takedown requests in this repository, in the spirit of [github/dmca](https://github.com/github/dmca), once the first request is received.
4. Open Granth is open to dialogue on alternative resolutions: attribution adjustment, licensing, or replacement with a public-domain alternative.

This is not a waiver of any defense Open Granth may hold, including fair use (17 U.S.C. § 107) and DMCA § 512(g) counter-notification. It is a statement of intent to act respectfully and promptly when a good-faith concern is raised.

## What the first release does and does not ship

**Ships:** Gurmukhi text of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, romanized transliteration, DSSK English translation for study, static site, search, verification tool, MCP server.

**Does not ship:** AI-generated interpretation presented as authoritative, paywalled access to Gurbani, ads, analytics cookies or cross-site tracking, or user accounts.

The English layer is imported from the DSSK translation asset in the Shabad OS database, attributed there to Dr. Sant S. Khalsa. It is included as an imported study aid, not as Open Granth's own interpretation. If you are concerned about a specific translation or interpretation, include the relevant passage, source, and URL/path in your request.
